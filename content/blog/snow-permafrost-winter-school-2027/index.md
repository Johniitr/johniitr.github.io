We are delighted to announce the **Snow–Permafrost Interactions Winter School: Processes, Measurements, and
1D Thermo-Hydrologic Modelling**, hosted by the [University of Trento](https://www.unitn.it/en) and co-hosted
by [Eurac Research](https://www.eurac.edu/en) in Bolzano, with the support of the
[European Geosciences Union (EGU)](https://www.egu.eu/).

The school will take place in Trento in **mid-February 2027**. Over one week, early-career scientists will
gain a rigorous, quantitative understanding of how the snowpack controls ground temperatures and permafrost,
from field observations to physically based 1D models of coupled heat and water flow in frozen ground.

> [!NOTE] At a glance
> - **When:** one week in mid-February 2027 (exact dates to follow)
> - **Where:** University of Trento, Trento, Italy
> - **Hosts:** University of Trento, with Eurac Research (Bolzano) as co-host
> - **Format:** four full teaching days, plus an optional field day in the Alps
> - **For:** early-career scientists, such as PhD students and postdocs
> - **Supported by:** the European Geosciences Union (EGU)

---

## Who should apply

PhD students, postdocs and other early-career scientists working on snow, permafrost, hydrology, soil physics
or mountain hazards. Whether you collect field data and want to model it, or build models and want to
understand the data behind them, the school is designed for you.
